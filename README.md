# kloun

**One app for everything you watch, read and listen to.** Movies, shows, anime, live TV, manga, comics, books, light novels, radio, podcasts and music, all in one library on Android phones, tablets and TVs.

kloun comes empty. It has no built-in sources, no accounts, no ads and no tracking. You add the sources and servers you want, and everything stays on your device.

## ✨ Key features

### 📚 One library for everything
- Video, live TV, manga and comics, books and novels, and audio, all side by side
- Watch later, Watching and Watched lists, favorites, and half-star ratings
- Search your library by title, tag, type, category or language
- A Continue row that picks up where you left off
- Notifications when saved titles get new chapters or episodes

### 🔌 Bring your own sources
- **Stremio add-ons**, working together just like in Stremio
- **Aniyomi, Tachiyomi and Mihon extensions** for anime and manga
- **LNReader plugins** for light novels
- **IPTV playlists** for live TV
- **Book catalogs (OPDS)**, such as Project Gutenberg or your Calibre library
- **Internet Archive** films, classic TV, cartoons, audiobooks, concerts and books
- **YouTube channels** you choose, in YouTube's own player
- **PeerTube** instances, **web comics** and **blogs**

### 🏠 Your own servers and files
- **Jellyfin, Emby and Plex** for movies and shows, with progress sent back to the server
- **Komga, Kavita and Suwayomi** for comics and manga
- **Audiobookshelf** for audiobooks and podcasts
- **Navidrome, Subsonic and Jellyfin** music
- **Network folders** over SMB, WebDAV and FTP, plus **DLNA** servers on your Wi-Fi
- Your own **EPUB, PDF, CBZ and text** files

### 🎬 A great player
- Fullscreen, picture in picture, a mini player and background audio
- Subtitles from add-ons, the video itself or your own files
- **Automatic subtitle sync** that listens to the audio and lines them up
- **Translate subtitles** into your language, right on the device
- **Read subtitles aloud**, with your choice of voice, speed and pitch
- Your audio language first, and a **Find one that plays** button that tries streams for you
- Torrent and debrid playback, with an option to allow torrents only through a VPN

### 📖 A reader for every format
- Manga in webtoon scroll, left to right or right to left
- EPUB and text books with flowing text, a table of contents, and light, sepia or dark pages
- Download chapters and books to read offline

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Profiles and kids mode
- Up to eight profiles, each with their own library, history and languages
- PIN locks, plus a master PIN for grown-ups
- **Kids profiles** with their own sources, adult titles always hidden, kid-safe YouTube search
- **Screen time** with a daily allowance, a bedtime and allowed hours

### 📺 Made for Android TV too
- The whole app works with a remote
- **Type on your phone**: scan a QR code to fill in text boxes on the TV from your phone

### 🔒 Private and safe
- No account, ads, telemetry or cloud. Everything stays on your device
- Extensions run in an isolated sandbox and only reach the sites you approve
- Passwords and keys are encrypted on the device
- Adult titles are blurred, hidden or shown, as you choose
- DNS over HTTPS and incognito mode

### 🎨 Make it yours
- Dark, light, Material You or AMOLED themes, with ten accent colors
- Glass or solid panels, rounded or square corners, and font and text size choices
- English, Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, Spanish and Hindi, with full right-to-left layouts

### 🔗 Stay in sync
- Track your progress on **AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Simkl and Trakt**
- Full backups, including automatic ones to a folder you choose
- **In-app updates** straight from this page

## 📥 Install

Download the APK for your device from the [latest release](https://github.com/JoEusebe/Kloun-app/releases/latest):

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| `Kloun-*-arm64-v8a.apk` | Almost every phone from the last several years |
| `Kloun-*-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Older 32-bit phones and many TV boxes |
| `Kloun-*-x86_64.apk` / `x86.apk` | Emulators and x86 devices |
| `Kloun-*-universal.apk` | Any device, if you're not sure (larger) |

Requires Android 8.0 or later. Once it's installed, kloun updates itself from **Settings → Update kloun**.

## ⚠️ Note

kloun is a player and reader. It doesn't host, include or recommend any content. You're responsible for the sources you add and for following the laws where you live.
